ABUNADIER ONLINE CLASS PROGRAM

The Abunadier Online Class Program aims to make learning accessible to every child by providing educational opportunities, learning materials, and online classes that help children develop their knowledge and skills.

We offer the following programs:

1. Free Learning Materials and Lessons
We provide free lessons and learning materials that children can use to practice and improve their knowledge at home, with the guidance and support of their parents.

2. Weekend Online Classes
We offer regular online classes every Saturday and Sunday, from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM. Registration is required for children who wish to participate in these classes.

3. Arabic Language Tutorial
We also offer Arabic language tutorials for those who are interested in learning the Arabic language. Students may choose from the available schedules provided by the administrator.

Our Commitment

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn. Through this program, we aim to support children, encourage continuous learning, and help parents become active partners in their children’s education.
